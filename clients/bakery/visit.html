Parking and transit

Free street parking on Franklin and on 21st Avenue behind the shop. The nearest light rail stop is two blocks north. There is a step-free entrance on the Franklin Boulevard side and the counter is at wheelchair height.

Picking up an order

Custom cake pickups happen Tuesday through Saturday during shop hours. Come to the counter and give the name on the order. Payment is taken at pickup by card or cash; nothing is charged when you submit the form.

Running late?

Call the shop. We will hold a finished cake in the case until close on your pickup day, and we would much rather hear from you than guess.

Have a question first?

Questions about a cake design, an allergy, or a large order are best asked before you place the order, and the phone is the fastest way.
