Menu

Prices are current as of July 2026. Cakes are made to order; everything else is baked daily and sold until it runs out, which on Saturdays can be well before noon.

Custom cakes

All custom cakes include your choice of vanilla, chocolate, tres leches, or almond, with buttercream or whipped filling, and lettering in any colour we can mix. We need 72 hours' notice, and a week is better during December and around graduation.
