Two tracks. One diploma. Taught by people who do the work.

Ironwood is a tuition-free public charter school where students graduate with both a high school diploma and real standing in a technology or skilled-trades career, having learned from instructors who still work in those fields.

Apply to Ironwood Schedule a tour

What makes Ironwood different

Most schools ask a student to choose between preparing for college and preparing for a trade. Ironwood treats that as a false choice. Every student takes a core academic load alongside a chosen track, and both tracks lead to paid work-based learning before graduation.

STEM track

Computer science, robotics, and cybersecurity, sequenced across four years so a student leaves with a portfolio and industry certifications rather than a transcript alone.

Skilled Trades track

Electrical, HVAC, welding, and automotive pathways taught in shop settings, with hours that count toward apprenticeship requirements after graduation.

Work-based learning

Internships, apprenticeships, and co-op placements are built into the upper grades, so students enter the workforce with a real reference, not just a résumé.

College credit before you graduate

Ironwood students earn dual-enrollment credit through Austin Community College while still in high school, and qualifying students can follow a bridge pathway into the University of Texas at Austin. Choosing a trade track does not close the college door; several of our welding and HVAC students carry the heaviest dual-enrollment loads in the school.
